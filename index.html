Better algorithms.
Broader possibilities.

Bringing the tools of molecular quantum chemistry to extended systems, with a focus on accuracy and computational efficiency.

01

Periodic exact exchange

Hybrid DFT / Tensor hypercontraction
02

Coulomb & integral algorithms

Combining range separation, density fitting, Fourier transforms, and integral screening to accelerate all-electron periodic calculations.

Gaussian bases / Scientific software
03

Correlated materials

Collaborating on many-body methods and energy decomposition to connect electronic structure with the properties and interactions of solids.

03 / About

Theory meets computation.

I’m a Ph.D. student in Chemistry and Chemical Biology at Harvard University, working with Prof. Joonho Lee. My research centers on efficient electronic structure algorithms for condensed-phase systems.

I’m interested in the connection between physical theory, numerical algorithms, and scientific software: how a better computational approach can make new questions about materials practical to explore.

Before joining the Lee Group in December 2022, I earned my B.S. at MIT, where I worked with Prof. Yogesh Surendranath on electrochemical systems.
